Riftbound

The League of Legends card game has a home on the river — we're an official Riftbound Gaming Network store with weekly Nexus Nights.

Plus One Piece, Gundam, and Digimon on the shelves, a wall of Squishmallows, sleeves, deck boxes, binders — and snacks and drinks so nobody has to leave mid-game.

The shop

The case

A counter full of singles

A long glass case of singles, slabs, and graded vintage — from current chase cards back to WOTC-era Pokémon — with row boxes of sorted rares behind the counter.

The hall

A real play hall

A dedicated event room with rows of playmat-topped tables, Ravnica guild banners overhead, mana symbols and jolly rogers on the walls, and an events calendar board by the door.

The wall

Sealed floor to ceiling

Booster boxes, collection boxes, and anniversary sets across every game we stock — Pokémon banners from the Neo days hanging above the new stuff.

The extras

Snacks, drinks & plush

A drink cooler and snack bar in the play hall, and a shelf of Squishmallows and Pokémon plush watching over the tables.

Singles & the case

Chasing a specific card? Browse the binders and display case in store — or send us a want list and we'll check the case for you.

The hold policy, plain and simple: if we pull cards for you, we hold them for one week. No pick-up and no word by then, and they go back in the case.
